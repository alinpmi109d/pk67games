Common PK67 Deposit Problems and Solutions

Deposit Deducted but Balance Not Updated

If money has left your payment account but your balance has not changed, avoid making another payment immediately. Save your receipt, check the transaction status, and contact support through a verified channel.

Incorrect Payment Details

An incorrect recipient number, account number, or reference can delay a transaction or send money to the wrong destination. If this happens, contact your payment provider promptly.

Payment Pending

A pending transaction may result from network interruptions, provider processing, or additional verification requirements. Check the status in your wallet or banking application before taking further action.

Deposit Rejected

A rejected transaction may involve insufficient funds, payment limits, incorrect information, or provider restrictions. Review the error message and contact the relevant support team if necessary.
